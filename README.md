# SSENSE Womenswear Information Architecture Redesign

An information architecture evaluation and redesign of the SSENSE womenswear shopping experience — focused on navigation depth, label clarity, findability, and accessibility.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Evaluate how shoppers navigate the SSENSE womenswear catalog and redesign the information architecture so people can find what they want faster, with less confusion.

## 👤 My Role

Planned and executed the full IA research cycle — from study design through analysis to annotated prototype recommendations.

## 🔬 Methods

- **Tree testing** — measured whether users could find items in the existing navigation structure
- **Task analysis** — broke down core shopping tasks to identify friction points
- **Open & closed card sorting** — uncovered users' mental models for category labels and groupings
- **Figma prototyping** — built annotated prototypes reflecting the recommended IA

## 📦 Key Deliverables

- Tree testing & card sorting study plans and results
- Task analysis documentation
- Annotated Figma prototypes of the redesigned navigation
- IA recommendations targeting:
  - **Findability** — can shoppers locate categories intuitively?
  - **Task efficiency** — fewer steps to reach a product
  - **Accessibility** — navigation that works for everyone

## 💡 Outcome

Delivered evidence-backed IA recommendations grounded in real user behavior data — not just best-practice heuristics.
